# CV writer research notes

- Coursera Staff, “Resume Sections: Key Categories and What to Include to Stand Out” (updated Aug 11, 2026): https://www.coursera.org/articles/resume-sections
  - Core sections: header/contact, experience, skills, education.
  - Optional: summary/objective, certifications, hobbies.
  - Header commonly includes name, city, phone, email, and website/portfolio.
  - Experience should capture title, company, dates, major tasks, and impact; action words and quantified achievements help.
  - References are generally better omitted from the CV and shared when requested.

- NACE, Kevin Gray, “The Key Skills Employers Seek on College Students’ Resumes” (Apr 20, 2026): https://www.naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/candidate-selection/the-key-skills-employers-seek-on-college-students-resumes
  - NACE Job Outlook 2026 Spring Update survey; 185 respondents, including 142 NACE employer members.
  - Employers commonly look for teamwork, problem-solving, and communication; the page reports 85% of employers look for teamwork skills.
  - Employers want evidence/examples of skills, not only a list.

Implementation implication: keep the existing compact sample layout, add optional portfolio/LinkedIn, certifications, languages, and achievement prompts; encourage action-led, evidence-based responsibilities. Do not make references a required CV section; keep it optional for the supplied local sample format.
