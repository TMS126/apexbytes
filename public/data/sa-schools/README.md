# South African school and town data

## Schools

Source: Department of Basic Education (DBE) EMIS national ordinary schools
masterlist, 2023 release.

- Portal: https://www.education.gov.za/Programmes/EMIS/EMISDownloads.aspx
- ArcGIS item: https://geo-portal-sa-educ.hub.arcgis.com/pages/master-list
- Downloaded workbook: https://sa-educ.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/af3c747dafd644e1a41475fe92f360e6/data

The nine province files contain open ordinary schools with name, town, phase and
sector. The 2023 release listed 24,981 rows; after removing closed/blank and
duplicate labels, 24,941 unique open school labels remain. This is a 2023
snapshot, not a live 2026 roll. Users can type a school that is missing or has
been renamed.

## Towns

Source: Wikipedia, "List of populated places in South Africa"
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_populated_places_in_South_Africa),
cleaned of address fragments and supplemented with major towns. It is a
convenience list for the CV city dropdown, not an official register. Users can
type any town or village that is missing.
